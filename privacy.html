WHOOP Intelligence

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · Independent personal wellness analytics app (development version)

About the app

WHOOP Intelligence is independent and not affiliated with WHOOP. Connecting an account will be optional and require your authorization. The public website now offers an optional email sign-in managed by Supabase Authentication. Signing in processes your email address and authentication metadata. WHOOP health-data synchronization is not yet enabled.

Data we plan to process

Once connection is enabled and you consent, authorized scopes may permit access to WHOOP profile information, recovery metrics (including resting heart rate and heart rate variability), sleep, daily strain, workouts and body measurements. Account identifiers, OAuth tokens, sync metadata and derived metrics may be stored to provide the service. We will never request your WHOOP password.

Use of information

To connect your account, synchronize approved data, calculate trends and display wellness insights. These insights are informational and not medical advice. We do not sell personal health data or use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

The planned application backend uses Supabase. Health records and access credentials are not stored in the public GitHub Pages website. WHOOP is the data source and GitHub hosts this public informational site. Tokens must be protected server-side. Production access is not enabled at this time.

Retention, access and deletion

When live service launches, authorized records will be retained while needed to provide your connected service, subject to any further documented retention limits. You may request access or deletion through the contact channel below; account ownership will be verified before acting. You may revoke WHOOP authorization through available connected-app controls. Self-service deletion of WHOOP Intelligence's stored WHOOP records and connection tokens is available from the My data & privacy page after signing in. This does not delete your underlying WHOOP account, nor does it confirm remote WHOOP token revocation. The current self-service JSON export is capped at 1,000 records.

International processing

Backend data is intended to be processed in the EU via the configured Supabase region. Some service-provider operations may involve other locations.

Policy changes

We will update this policy as the implementation changes and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy requests, please contact the project maintainer. Do not post personal health information or credentials in a public GitHub issue; request a private contact method first.
